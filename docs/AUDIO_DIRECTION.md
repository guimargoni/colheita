# Audio Direction — Warm, Restful, Memorable

## Sonic identity
Cozy, optimistic, pastoral, warm, distinctive and not musically intrusive. Themes should be memorable enough to evoke the game years later.

## Background music
Use an adaptive playlist with seamless looping, gentle transitions and controlled loudness.
- Morning/day: gentle acoustic guitar, piano, flute, soft plucks.
- Evening/night: reduced instrumentation and calm melodic variation.
- Rain: atmosphere layers and low-key arrangement.
- Halloween: playful mysterious instrumentation, not frightening.
- Winter: bells, warm pads and delicate melody.
- Social visit: subtle alternate arrangement.
No abrupt restarting upon opening menus.

## Soundscape
Wind, birds, insects, rain, small animals, water, occasional farm ambience. Layer sounds with variability and reasonable polyphony caps.

## Sound effects
Planting, watering, harvesting, coins, shop, crafting, unlock, pet bark/chase/bite, event reward, panel interactions. For repeated actions vary samples/pitch subtly and throttle stacked sounds.

## Technical audio
Audio buses for master/music/ambient/SFX/UI; saved sliders; mute; reduced SFX preference; platform respect for backgrounding and mobile audio expectations; crossfade tracks; loop seamlessly; graceful missing track fallback.

## Production pipeline
Codex can code generative compositions, write MIDI arrangements and audio integration, but final production may involve an approved audio generation/composition tool and listening review. Verify model/license grant allows commercial game distribution and sublicensing as embedded game audio; never imitate copyrighted melodies or use unlicensed compositions. Keep provenance and license manifest. Master and test loops on real speakers/phone/headphones.

## Acceptance
At least 20 minutes of mixed play should feel comfortable with music enabled. No ear-fatiguing repeated UI effects. Music remains a background companion rather than obscuring interaction feedback. All meaningful actions work with sound disabled.
