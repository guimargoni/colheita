# Original Game Research Notes

Purpose: preserve the *design essence* of the classic social farming experience without copying proprietary implementation or presentation.

## Verified/high-confidence legacy mechanics
Research sources and surviving descriptions consistently point to:
- real-time crop growth;
- planting seeds/flowers;
- watering/care;
- pests/weeds or similar nuisances;
- harvesting and selling through storage/depot;
- coins and progression;
- many initially locked farm plots;
- purchasing/unlocking more farm capacity;
- animals and animal production;
- farm customization/decorations/scenery;
- visiting friends' farms;
- helping friends;
- placing or encountering nuisances;
- stealing some farm production;
- guardian dogs/other guardians that could stop thieves;
- feeding guardians to keep them effective;
- premium currency/VIP/special content in the historical product.

## What appears especially important to player memory
Community recollections emphasize:
- stealing from friends;
- the social reaction to theft;
- timed real-world growth;
- customization;
- animals;
- tending plants;
- nostalgic simplicity.

## Design interpretation
The project should not become a Stardew-like walking/life RPG by accident.

The distinguishing identity is:
**persistent asynchronous farm + customization + playful social interference.**

Everything new should reinforce that identity.

## Historical mechanics that need modernization
### Theft
Keep it, but add:
- caps;
- protected owner yield;
- cooldowns;
- logs;
- beginner protection;
- block/privacy controls.

### Nuisances
Keep playful sabotage, but ensure it cannot destroy valuable progress.

### Premium monetization
Do not recreate aggressive legacy pay advantages. Prefer cosmetics, themes, optional speedups with caps, passes/events and convenience.

### Social graph
Old games inherited Orkut friends. Modern version needs an in-game friend/follow system and platform integrations as optional adapters.

## Potential modern additions that fit
- processing buildings;
- order board;
- crop/animal mastery;
- collections;
- farm themes;
- cooperative neighborhoods;
- event framework;
- reactions/activity feed;
- photo/share mode;
- visitor guestbook/reactions;
- asynchronous competitions based on themed goals.

## Features that do not fit early
- combat;
- huge open world;
- mandatory avatar walking;
- heavy narrative RPG;
- realtime chat;
- player-run auction house.

These increase scope without strengthening the core nostalgia/social loop.
