# Ethical F2P Monetization and Balance

Status: strategy hypothesis to validate through prototypes and real player feedback, not guaranteed commercial projections.

## Business objective
Generate sustainable revenue while the free game remains complete and genuinely enjoyable.

## Monetization priority
1. Cosmetic farm themes, premium decorations, pet appearances, animation packs and ambience/music themes.
2. Optional seasonal pass with genuinely valuable free track and premium cosmetic track.
3. Optional convenience offers: extra cosmetic loadout slots, decoration storage convenience, limited capped speedups.
4. Transparent direct-support founder bundles and rotating cosmetic packs.
5. Optional rewarded ads on mobile only if research/testing shows they fit; no forced interstitials in core loop. PC/Steam storefront should not depend on mobile ads.

## Historic inspiration, not literal replication
Classic farming social games commonly used virtual coins and premium currencies for exclusive/convenience content, including time acceleration or special items. Exact historical price points and mechanisms require primary-source verification before being represented as confirmed facts. Do not duplicate old offers blindly.

## Currencies
Coins: earned through core play, necessary for crops/animals/land.
Premium currency: optional purchase and limited fair-gameplay rewards, spent on cosmetics and carefully bounded convenience.
Seasonal tokens: earned by playing events; clear expiry/claim policies.

## Purchase UX
Clear local currency totals and item benefits, previews for cosmetics, confirmations for premium spend, receipt/reconciliation, restores, refunds and fraud monitoring. No deceptive bundles, artificial urgency, fake discounts or concealed odds.

## Fair-play guardrails
- No paid theft advantage or unbeatable guardian.
- Free players can achieve meaningful levels, unlock land and participate in events.
- Speedups capped so they cannot break social or event leaderboards.
- No paid-only crop with substantially better progression economy.
- Do not monetize essential security, recovery, block/report or accessibility.
- Do not sell random paid loot boxes at launch.

## Balance model
Every item/crop has expected coin value per hour of grow time, unlock level, attention cost, care opportunities, theft exposure, yield variability, inventory footprint, order utility and event interactions.
Sinks: seeds, land, feed, production costs, crafting, normal decorations.
Sources: harvest sales, order rewards, achievements, quests, events.
Simulate progression without purchases at short (5-minute), medium (20-minute) and long (60-minute) daily play profiles.
Track median wealth, inflation, inventory accumulation, bottlenecks, return-rate and frustration before raising prices or timers.

## Avoid premium pressure
Do not impose manufactured inconvenience to sell relief. Premium options should feel like added expression or nice shortcuts, never removal of an intentionally painful mechanic.

## Analytics and commercial model
Track MAU/DAU, D1/D7/D30 retention, payer conversion, ARPDAU, ARPPU, churn, LTV, CAC, margin net of store fees/taxes/refunds/operations, ad opt-in and event participation. Cohort analysis required before scaling paid acquisition.

Monthly gross revenue approximation: MAU × payer conversion × average spend per paying user, plus optional ad revenues. Net developer proceeds may be materially lower after platform fees, taxes, refunds, payment processing, hosting and marketing.

Never portray illustrative assumptions as observed traction.

## Platform payments
Steam in-game purchases use Steam's authorized microtransaction/Steam Wallet flow and reliable receipt reconciliation.
Android and iOS must comply with applicable store and jurisdiction-specific digital goods purchase/billing rules at shipping date. Integrate platform adapters, not client-trusted grants.

## Live revenue operations
Releases should have a transparent shop calendar, price history, safe rollback, refunds policy and support path. Seasonal returns can remix cosmetics while preserving fair expectations.

## Validation
Run qualitative playtests first. Validate whether players like cosmetics/themes enough to pay voluntarily. Measure retention and economy health before aggressive monetization tuning.
